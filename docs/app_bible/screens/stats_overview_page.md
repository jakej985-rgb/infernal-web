# Presentation — StatsOverviewPage

## Purpose

Presents shop owners with dynamic financial trends, revenue growth models, client densities, and appointment completion rates.

## Widgets / Visual Style

* **Overview Metric Grid**: Displays key highlights (Total Revenue, Total Clients, Completed Appointments, and Upcoming Sessions) inside glow cards.
* **Translucent Revenue Chart**: High-end bar chart displaying weekly or monthly revenue trendlines with gold-to-ember color gradients and soft halos.
* **Status Distribution Indicators**: Linear progress trackers showing status percentages (completed, scheduled, cancelled).
