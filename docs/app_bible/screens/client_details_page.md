# Presentation — ClientDetailsPage

## Purpose

Presents comprehensive biographical and booking history information for a specific client.

## Widgets / Visual Style

* **Client Bio Header**: Presents client pronouns, phone, and email in stylized, high-contrast rows.
* **Interactive Body Map**: Clickable vector body chart to visual and chart tattoo placements on the collector.
* **History Tab List**: List of past, current, and upcoming appointments conducted with this client.

## Uses Classes

* **Client** ([Client](../classes/client.md)): Explored model.
