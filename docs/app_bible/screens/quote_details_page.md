# Presentation — QuoteDetailsPage

## Purpose

Presents details of a drafted price estimate, with tools to generate booking proposals.

## Widgets / Visual Style

* **Estimate Cards**: Highlighted listings of all estimate scores, dimension values, styles, and color notes.
* **Inline Actions**: Buttons to immediately schedule an appointment from this quote or share details with a client.

## Uses Classes

* **Quote** ([Quote](../classes/quote.md)): Displayed sheet.
